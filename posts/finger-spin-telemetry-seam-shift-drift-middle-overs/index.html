Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between spin turn and aerodynamic drift?

Drift occurs through the air before the ball hits the ground due to aerodynamic airflow differences, whereas turn occurs when the ball grips the surface and changes direction off the pitch.

Why is high release RPM essential for drift?

Higher revolutions create a stronger gyroscopic axis and sustained Magnus pressure differential, enabling the ball to deviate sideways through the air.
